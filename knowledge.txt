## 1. What Acme CRM Is
- Acme CRM is a (fictional) sales engagement platform: it helps sales teams run
  multichannel outreach — email, calls, SMS, WhatsApp and LinkedIn — from one place.
- Reps build automated sequences, get a daily task list, and see what's working.

## 2. If asked "is Acme CRM a CRM?" — DON'T give a flat yes or no
- Explain that it runs outreach and keeps activity in sync with the CRM they already use,
  then ask what they're trying to fix.

## 3. Core Problem It Solves
- Reps waste hours on manual follow-ups across too many tools.
- Acme CRM automates the follow-up, keeps everything in one inbox, and shows managers
  which messages actually book meetings.

## 4. Core Features (what each does)
- Sequences: multi-step, multichannel cadences with automatic follow-ups.
- Unified inbox: replies from every channel in one place.
- Dialer: click-to-call with call notes and recordings.
- Task list: a prioritized daily to-do for every rep.
- Templates + A/B tests: find the subject lines and messages that convert.
- Analytics: reply, meeting and conversion rates per sequence and per rep.
- AI assist: drafts personalized first lines and summarizes replies.

## 5. Channels Supported
- Email, phone, SMS, WhatsApp, LinkedIn tasks.

## 6. Integrations
- CRMs: two-way sync with popular CRMs.
- Calendars: Google Calendar and Outlook for meeting booking.
- Comms: Slack notifications.
- Automation: Zapier and an open REST API.

## 7. Plans & Pricing (fictional — per user, monthly or annual; annual saves more)
- Starter — ~$29/user/mo: sequences, email channel, basic analytics.
- Growth — ~$59/user/mo: all channels, dialer, A/B tests, CRM sync.
- Enterprise — ~$99/user/mo (billed annually): everything in Growth plus SSO,
  custom roles and a dedicated success manager.
- These are starting prices — share them if asked, and note the demo gives the exact
  quote for their team size and plan.
